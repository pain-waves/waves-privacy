### Common questions

**Why is a task showing a dashed outline?**
That's an estimate Waves guessed because the source has no duration field.
Tap the block and confirm or change the time.

**Why didn't a new calendar event appear?**
Waves reads what your device has already downloaded. If you added the event on
another device, open the Calendar app once to force a sync, then use
Menu → Refresh sources in Waves.

**Where did my all-day event go?**
All-day events have no honest duration, so they wait on a shelf. Tap the ◇ badge
under the date and set how long it really takes.

## Privacy Policy

Waves does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

The app has no server and no user accounts. Calendar and reminder data is read
on your device solely to display your workload, and is never transmitted
anywhere. All tasks, projects, settings, and history are stored only on your
device and are permanently removed when you delete the app.

Waves does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking of any kind.

Waves reads your calendars and reminders. It never modifies them.
