# Privacy Policy for Waves

Waves does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

The app has no server and no user accounts. Calendar and reminder data is read
on your device solely to display your workload, and is never transmitted
anywhere. All tasks, projects, settings, and history are stored only on your
device and are permanently removed when you delete the app.

Waves does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking of any kind.

Waves reads your calendars and reminders. It never modifies them.
